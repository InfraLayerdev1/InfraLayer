# AI Assistant Handoff

## What this is

A company knowledge assistant that answers questions from approved documents.

## How it works

Documents
→ Processing
→ Vector Search
→ AI Response

## Client Responsibilities

Provide:
- Company policies
- SOP documents
- FAQs
- Internal guides

## Testing

Before launch:
- Test common questions
- Test missing information
- Verify answers

## Maintenance

Update documents when policies change.
