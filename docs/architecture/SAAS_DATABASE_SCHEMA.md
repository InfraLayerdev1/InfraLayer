# SaaS Database Thinking

## Person

Question:

Who is this?

Example:

Evan

What does the system need to remember?

- Name
- Email
- Unique identity

Why?

Because the system must know who is asking.

---

## Company

Question:

Who owns the information?

Example:

Company A

What does the system need to remember?

- Company name
- Company identity

Why?

Because one company must not see another company's data.

---

## Relationship

Question:

What is this person's position inside a company?

Example:

Evan works at Company A.

What does the system need to know?

- Company
- Role
- Department
- Level

Why?

Because a person alone does not define access.

The relationship defines access.

---

## Knowledge

Question:

What information exists?

Example:

Pricing document.

What does the system need to know?

- Who owns it
- Which department owns it
- Who can read it

Why?

Because documents need protection.

---

## Permission

Question:

What is this person allowed to do?

Example:

Sales Manager can read sales documents.

Why?

Because roles are not enough.

Specific access rules are needed.
